A perfectly centred cylinder never sheds (in maths). At Re 150 a real cylinder makes a vortex street, but put it exactly in the middle of a symmetric channel and the computer has no reason to push the wake up or down, so the unstable steady wake survives forever. Shift it one cell (5% of its width) and the wobble grows like e^(σt) into a full street. Shift it five cells and it just gets there sooner: same street, St ≈ 0.17.

Real flows always have tiny disturbances, which is why real wakes always shed.
