Break the sound barrier. At Mach 0.5 every ring outruns the source. At Mach 1 the source keeps pace with its own rings and they pile up at the nose into one wall: the "barrier". At Mach 1.5 it escapes its own rings and their edges line up on a cone with sin θ = 1/M = 0.67, θ = 41.8°. At Mach 2 (Concorde's cruise) the cone narrows to 30°. The boom you hear is that cone sweeping over the ground.
