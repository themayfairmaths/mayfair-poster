Mach 0.5: the waves escape ahead of the source. Mach 1: they pile up into a wall - the sound barrier. Mach 1.5: the source outruns its own waves and they form a cone. That cone sweeping over the ground is the sonic boom. Cone angle: sin θ = 1/Mach.
