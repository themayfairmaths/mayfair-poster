One equation. Four behaviours. xₙ₊₁ = r·xₙ(1 − xₙ), drawn as a cobweb: up to the curve, across to the diagonal, repeat. r = 2.8: it spirals into the fixed point x* = 1 − 1/r, stable because the slope there |2 − r| < 1. r = 3.2: that slope passes −1 and a 2-cycle is born (0.513 ↔ 0.799). r = 3.5: four values. r = 3.9: chaos, no pattern at all. Same line of algebra, one knob.
