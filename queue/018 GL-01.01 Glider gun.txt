Conway's Game of Life has three rules: a dead cell with exactly 3 live neighbours is born, a live cell with 2 or 3 survives, everything else dies. A glider (5 cells) repeats its shape every 4 generations, one cell further on. The Gosper glider gun (1970) rebuilds itself every 30 generations and fires a new glider: 36 cells + 5 per glider, for ever. It won Conway's $50 prize for a pattern that grows without limit.
