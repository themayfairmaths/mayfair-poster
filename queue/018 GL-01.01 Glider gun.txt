Conway's Game of Life has three rules: a dead cell with exactly 3 live neighbours is born, a live cell with 2 or 3 survives, everything else dies. From those rules comes the Gosper glider gun (1970): a machine that fires a new glider every 30 generations, for ever.

Rendered in Python with exactly the same rules as the Mayfair Math Excel workbook. Free workbook coming soon - follow so you don't miss it.
