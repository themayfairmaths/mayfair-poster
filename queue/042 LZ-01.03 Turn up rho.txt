Change one number and watch it break. In the Lorenz equations ρ is how hard the system is driven. ρ = 0.5: everything dies out at the origin. ρ = 14: two rest points C± = (±√(β(ρ−1)), ±√(β(ρ−1)), ρ−1) appear and the path spirals into one. ρ = 21: chaos for a while, then it gives up. Above ρ = σ(σ+β+3)/(σ−β−1) = 24.74 nothing is stable: ρ = 28 is the famous butterfly. Push further and order comes back: ρ = 100 repeats every three loops, ρ = 160 is one big loop.
