3 cells, each 0 or 1, gives 8 patterns, so a rule is 8 output bits: exactly 256 rules. Here they all are. Mirror a rule (swap left and right) or swap black and white and you get an equivalent rule, so the 256 fall into just 88 families. Then run every rule from a random row, zip the result and sort by how badly it compresses: most rules are boring and only a handful are truly wild.
