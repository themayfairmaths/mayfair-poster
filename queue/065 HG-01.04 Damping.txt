Friction is the e^(−d t). With d = 0 the swing never shrinks and the pen fills the box. d = 0.004 halves the swing every 173 s: a spiral rose. d = 0.03 halves it every 23 s and after 100 s only 5% is left. Just the right friction makes the art.
