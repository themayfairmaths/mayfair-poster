How long can two pendulums agree? Pendulum B starts a tiny angle ε away from pendulum A; same equations, same solver. 0.001 rad apart: obviously different after 3.4 s. A millionth of a radian: 10.0 s. A billionth: 13.9 s. The gap grows roughly like ε·e^(λt), so every 1,000× better start buys only about 5 more seconds of agreement. That's the butterfly effect, on your desk.
