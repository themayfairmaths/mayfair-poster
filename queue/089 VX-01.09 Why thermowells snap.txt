Why thermowells snap. A thermowell is a probe tube sticking into pipe flow, and like a guitar string it has a natural frequency fₙ. The swirls shed off it at f = St·U/D. At half of fₙ it barely shakes (1.3× the static push). At 0.75 fₙ it's 2.3×. At 0.99 fₙ each swirl pushes just as the tube swings back: with 2% damping the shaking is about 24× the static push, and thousands of cycles later it cracks. Standards such as ASME PTC 19.3 TW keep shedding well below fₙ.
