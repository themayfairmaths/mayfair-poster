A thermowell is a little tube that sticks into a pipe to hold a temperature sensor. Flow past it sheds vortices, and each one gives it a sideways push. Speed the flow up and the shedding frequency climbs - when it reaches the thermowell's own natural frequency it locks in and shakes itself to fatigue failure. A broken thermowell caused the 1995 sodium leak at Japan's Monju reactor. (The flow is simulated; the natural frequency line is illustrative.)
