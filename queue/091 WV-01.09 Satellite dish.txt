Why dishes are parabolas. A flat wall just bounces straight waves back. Curve it into a parabola x = x_v − y²/4f and every path from far away, to the dish, to the focus has the same length, so all the reflections arrive in step and pile up into one bright spot, f = 30 cells out. Make the dish deeper (f = 15) and the focus moves closer. Satellite TV, radio telescopes and whisper dishes all put the receiver right there.
