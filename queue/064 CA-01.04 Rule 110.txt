Rule 110 = 01101110. From one cell it only grows to the left, over a repeating background called the ether. From a random start, most of the mess settles and what survives are gliders: diagonal stripes that travel. When gliders collide they vanish, pass through or make new ones, and Matthew Cook (2004) used those collisions to build logic. Rule 110 is Turing complete: 8 bits of rule, a whole computer.
