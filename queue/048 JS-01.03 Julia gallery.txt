Four values of c, four fractals. Rabbit (c = −0.123 + 0.745i): inside the Mandelbrot set, one piece. Dragon (−0.8 + 0.156i): just outside, so the orbit of 0 escapes, but only after 251 steps, which is why it looks connected. Spirals (0.285 + 0.01i), near the cusp. Seahorses (−0.7269 + 0.1889i): a hair outside, 430 steps to escape. The closer c is to the edge of the Mandelbrot set, the more detail you get.
