What the seabed does to a pipe's wake. The floor is a no-slip wall (u = v = 0), so it fights the swirls. Two diameters up, the pipe sheds a normal street. One diameter up, the lower swirls drag along the floor. Half a diameter up, shedding turns weak and one-sided. A quarter of a diameter up, a fast jet squirts through the gap and takes over. Subsea pipelines are designed around exactly this, because the gap changes the forces on the pipe.
