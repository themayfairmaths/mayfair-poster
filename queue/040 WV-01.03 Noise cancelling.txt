How noise-cancelling works, measured. Three probe points sit in the water and plot their height live. With both sources in step, A and C are loud and B is quiet. Flip source 2 by 180° (sin(θ + 180°) = −sin θ): A goes silent and B gets loud, but C, off to the side, barely changes. Cancelling only works in certain spots. On the centre line the two paths are equal, so the sum is exactly zero. That's why headphones do it right at your ear.
