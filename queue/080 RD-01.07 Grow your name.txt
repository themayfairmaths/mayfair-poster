Same letters, three recipes. We seed the grid with the word MATH (u = 0.5, v = 0.25 inside the letters, u = 1, v = 0 everywhere else) and run the Gray-Scott equations with three different (F, k) pairs. Coral (F 0.0545, k 0.062): the letters grow and branch. Spots (0.030, 0.062): they break into dots that split and spread. Maze (0.029, 0.057): the strokes stretch into stripes. The start is forgotten; the recipe decides.
