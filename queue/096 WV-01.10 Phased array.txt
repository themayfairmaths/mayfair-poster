Steer a beam without moving anything. Eight wave sources half a wavelength apart. All in step: the beam goes straight ahead. Delay each one by Δφ = 60° behind its neighbour and the beam tilts to sin θ = (Δφ/360°)·λ/d = 1/3, θ = 19.5°. Δφ = 120°: θ = 41.8°. Then change Δφ live from −120° to +120° and the beam sweeps across all four listening points. No moving parts, just timing: how 5G masts and modern radar steer.
