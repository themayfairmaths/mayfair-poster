A cylinder at Re 80 sheds a vortex street. Put a tiny second cylinder - one fifth of the diameter - in the right spot just behind it and the shedding switches off: the wake swing drops from 0.37 to 0.003. Move it half a diameter further back and the street comes back. Strykowski & Sreenivasan found this in experiments in 1990. (Run on a finer 480 × 192 grid - same scheme as the workbook.)
