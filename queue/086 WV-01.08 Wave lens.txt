Focus a wave with maths. Straight crests pass a lens where the wave speed drops to K·c. The lens is thickest in the middle, so the middle is held back most and the crests curve towards a focus, exactly like glass with refractive index n = 1/K. Make the glass slower (K = 0.5, n = 2) and the lensmaker's equation 1/f = (n − 1)(1/R₁ + 1/R₂) says the focus moves about 2× closer. That's what your glasses do.
