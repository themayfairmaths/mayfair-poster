Round a square's corners off bit by bit, from sharp (r = 0) to a full circle (r = D/2). The flow hugs a curved corner a little longer before letting go, so the wake narrows and the beat quickens: St = 0.134, 0.144, 0.149, 0.156. That's why rounded building corners and smooth cables shed differently from sharp ones.
