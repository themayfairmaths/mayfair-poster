Spin the triangle. Still, a swirl pair leaves about every 1,000 steps (St = 0.196). Slow spin: a triangle looks the same every 120°, so at 90° per 1000 steps the same shape comes round every 1,333 steps, close to the swirl rhythm, and the two rhythms tangle. Fast spin: the same shape every 167 steps, 6× faster than the swirls, so the wake only feels the average shape, which is round: St = 0.172, almost exactly a circle's 0.169.
