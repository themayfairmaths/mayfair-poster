Why do patterns form at all? Let u and v spread at the same speed and every bump dies away: no pattern. Make u spread 1.5 times faster and a maze appears; at 2 times (the workbook's Du = 0.2097, Dv = 0.105) it fills the grid. Alan Turing's 1952 idea: different spreading speeds turn a smooth mix into spots and stripes, perhaps even a leopard's coat.
