Small, medium, large. Same Re. Three cylinders, 10, 20 and 30 cells wide, each with the viscosity scaled (ν = UD/Re) so all three run at Re 150. The swirls scale with the cylinder and come off at the same rate in units of D/U: St = 0.150, 0.169 and 0.182 (the biggest one is squeezed a little by the channel walls). That's why a small model in a wind tunnel tells you about the real thing: match Re and you match the flow.
