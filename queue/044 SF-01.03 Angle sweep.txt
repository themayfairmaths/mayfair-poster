Sweep the turn from 132° to 137.7°. Every angle near a simple fraction of a turn makes arms: 135° = 3/8 makes 8 spokes, 8/21 makes 21 arms. As the fractions get more complicated the arms multiply and the gaps shrink, and right at 137.508° (between all the Fibonacci fractions 5/13, 8/21, 13/34 …) the arms vanish and the packing is the best of any angle. Plants found this number by evolution.
