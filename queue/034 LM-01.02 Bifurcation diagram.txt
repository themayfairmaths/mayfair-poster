The most famous picture in chaos. For every r, run the logistic map 600 times, then plot where it ends up. One line splits into 2, 4, 8 … then smears into chaos at r ≈ 3.57. The Lyapunov exponent λ = average of ln|r(1 − 2x)| measures it: below zero is order, above zero is chaos. And the splits come faster by the same ratio every time, δ = 4.669: Feigenbaum's constant, the same for any one-hump map.

Save this one: it's the map of chaos.
