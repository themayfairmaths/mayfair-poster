One rule makes a sunflower: each new seed turns 137.508° from the last and sits at radius r = c√n. The √n keeps the seeds evenly spread, because the disc's area grows by the same amount for every seed; try r = cn instead and the middle crowds while the edge empties. The angle is the golden angle, 360° × (1 − 1/φ) with φ = (1 + √5)/2, and the Fibonacci ratios 2/1, 3/2, 5/3, 8/5 … close in on φ.
