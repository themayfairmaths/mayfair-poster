A square box full of fluid. The lid slides right; the other three walls stay still. Start the lid and viscosity drags the fluid round into one big vortex, with two tiny counter-rotating eddies in the bottom corners (red). It settles after about 5 lid lengths. Then the test: Ghia, Ghia and Shin (1982) is the benchmark every CFD code is checked against. Our 96 × 96 solver gets a vortex strength of 0.1036 against their 0.1034, and the centre-line velocity lands within 0.01 U everywhere.
