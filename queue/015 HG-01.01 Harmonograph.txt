A harmonograph: two pendulums swing the pen left-right, two swing it up-down, and friction slowly shrinks every swing. x = Σ A sin(f t + p) e^(−d t). With no friction, frequencies 2 and 3 trace one closed Lissajous loop for ever. Add friction e^(−0.004 t) (half size every 173 s) and the loop spirals in. Detune the four pendulums slightly and the pairs beat.
