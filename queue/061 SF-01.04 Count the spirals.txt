Colour every 8th seed, then every 13th, 21st, 34th: each time you get a set of spirals, and the counts are Fibonacci numbers. Why? 13 × 137.508° ≈ 5 whole turns, 21 × 137.508° ≈ 8 whole turns, 34 × 137.508° ≈ 13 whole turns, so every 13th (or 21st, 34th) seed drifts slowly round into a spiral. Real sunflowers show 34 and 55, or 55 and 89. Go and count one.
