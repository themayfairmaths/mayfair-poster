One black cell, one rule. Each new cell looks at three cells above it (left, centre, right): 8 possible patterns, and the rule number 30 = 00011110 in binary gives the answer for each. The left side makes stripes, the right side chaos. The centre column has never been shown to repeat, and Mathematica used it to make random numbers. Flip one starting cell and the difference spreads in a cone, at most one cell per step.
