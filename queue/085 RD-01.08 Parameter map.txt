Every pattern in one picture. Here every cell gets its own recipe: the feed rate F rises left → right and the kill rate k rises bottom → top. Zoomed out, most of the map is boring: v starves (dark) or floods (flat yellow). Zoom in and spots, worms and holes sit side by side, blending at the borders. Zoom again and a change of F by just 0.01 flips spots into stripes. That thin band is where all the interesting shapes live.
