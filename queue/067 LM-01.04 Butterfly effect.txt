Population maths goes chaotic. At r = 4 the logistic map doubles any tiny error every step on average (λ = ln 2). Start two populations 0.001 apart: they're clearly different after 8 generations. A millionth apart: 17. Ten billionths apart: 30. Every 1,000× better measurement buys only about 10 more steps, and even a computer's 53 binary digits run out after about 53 steps.
