When does a pendulum turn chaotic? Start both arms at 30° and its path in angle space retraces a neat band. At 100° patches of order and chaos mix. At 170° it flips over the top again and again and scribbles over everything. Then every start at once: 57,600 pendulums, coloured by when they first flip. Grey ones never have the energy (2cos θ₁ + cos θ₂ > 1). The border between calm and chaos is a fractal.
