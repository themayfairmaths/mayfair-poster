Double the wind, double the note. Same wire, same air, three wind speeds. At half speed the swirls leave every 246 time units; at three-quarter speed every 158; at full speed every 118. The frequency f = 1/T climbs in a straight line with the wind because f = St·U/D and St barely changes (≈ 0.17). That's the hum of power lines and the whistle of branches in a gale: Aeolian tones.
